How large is the home?

The current area schedule presents approximately 3,476 conditioned square feet and approximately 4,650 square feet total under roof. The garage is the only enclosed unconditioned portion; covered porches are included under roof but not in conditioned living area. Current construction plans, specifications and the executed purchase agreement control final dimensions.

How is the bedroom plan organized?

The home is marketed with four bedrooms and 3.5 baths. The primarily one-level plan includes a private owner’s wing, guest accommodations and a conditioned suite above the garage.

What can a buyer still personalize?

Time-sensitive HVAC, plumbing, gas, wet-area, electrical, smart-home, appliance and outdoor-kitchen decisions may remain available when approved before the applicable rough-in is completed.

Is a generator available?

An optional 26 kW Generac whole-home package with automatic transfer-switch equipment is available, subject to final coordination and written approval.

What warranty is included?

The home will be enrolled in the Quality Builders Warranty 10-year new-home limited-warranty program. The agreement delivered at closing controls all terms and procedures.

When is completion anticipated?

The current estimated completion period is approximately six to seven months from the present construction stage, subject to construction conditions and approved changes.
